# About Us

ReSINC stands for **Research in Security, Intelligence & Cyberthreat**. 

We are an independent research group dedicated to exploring the depths of cybersecurity. Founded in 2024, our group has steadily grown into a hub for cutting-edge security research, focusing on threat intelligence, binary exploitation, and reverse engineering.

Whether you're a seasoned professional or a student looking to get into the field, we believe in sharing knowledge and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in infosec.

## Our Goal

At ReSINC, our primary goal is to advance the state of cybersecurity through rigorous research, open collaboration, and intelligence sharing. 

We aim to:

1. **Identify** emerging cyber threats before they become mainstream.
2. **Educate** the community on best practices for secure software development.
3. **Develop** open-source tools to assist in threat hunting and intelligence gathering.

Together, we can build a more secure digital ecosystem.
