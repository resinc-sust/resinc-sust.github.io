About Us🔗

ReSINC stands for Research in Security, Intelligence & Cyberthreat.

We are the official Cyber Security Group of Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Bangladesh, dedicated to exploring the depths of cybersecurity. Founded in 2022, our group has steadily grown into a hub for cutting-edge security research, focusing on threat intelligence, binary exploitation, and reverse engineering.

Whether you're a seasoned professional or a student looking to get into the field, we believe in sharing knowledge and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in infosec.

Our Goal🔗

At ReSINC, our primary goal is to conduct cutting-edge cybersecurity research.

We aim to:

  1. Discover zero-day vulnerabilities, contribute CVE findings, and participate in bug bounty programs and open security research through responsible disclosure.
  2. Deliver quality penetration testing services.
  3. Publish cutting-edge security research, write-ups, and open-source tools for the community.
  4. Educate freshmen and sophomore newcomers, building a strong security foundation from day one.

Together, we can build a more secure digital ecosystem.
